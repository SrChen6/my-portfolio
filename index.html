About

I'm a student in the CFIS double-degree programme at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, combining Physics Engineering with Data Science.

On the physics side I work on electromagnetism, transmission lines and optics; on the data side, machine learning, computer vision and statistical modelling. What excites me most is where the two meet: physics-informed models, simulations and visualizations that make complex systems intuitive.
